# Bolna and India voice-AI hackathons: what wins and what Bolna values

Research date: 2026-10-18. Scope: India, 2024–2026. Every claim has a URL. Anything without a source is in Gaps.

## Q1. Bolna Buildathon 2026: official page, judges, rubric, tracks, prizes, FAQ, updates

### Takeaway
The official page (re-fetched 2026-10-18) matches the local `hackathon-details.md`. There are no tracks and no published rubric. The only named judge is Grey Baker, who interviews the winner. Selection is Bolna-run: Bolna reviews submissions, holds interviews on Nov 5–7, and the top 3 pitch on Nov 20. Bolna staff will decide the shortlist, and a Symphony crowd or jury will hear the final pitches.

### Cited Findings
- Fully virtual build from Oct 15 to 31, 2026. Submissions are due Oct 31. Shortlisted teams get interview invites on Nov 3, interviews run Nov 5–7, finalists are announced Nov 8, and the Grand Finale is Nov 20 at Abhyas Hall, The Lalit Ashok, Bengaluru. Only the top 3 travel, and their travel is covered. — [Bolna Buildathon page](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon)
- No tracks: "any idea" with a voice component, either a new product or a voice layer on an existing product. The example ideas are a kids' voice tutor, a phone helpdesk for a local clinic, a voice layer on an existing app, and a hands-free tool for drivers or field workers. — [Bolna Buildathon page](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon)
- Prizes total ₹1.2 lakh: 1st gets ₹50k plus a YC interview with Grey Baker, 2nd gets ₹40k, 3rd gets ₹30k, and the top 10 get Symphony tickets. No judging criteria or rubric are published, and no judge other than Grey Baker is named. — [Bolna Buildathon page](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon)
- The submission form asks for a description of at most 200 characters, a GitHub, live app or demo link, and a pitch deck or video link. — [Submit page](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon/submit) (via local hackathon-details.md)
- Grey Baker is now a YC **General Partner**, promoted from Visiting Partner on June 16, 2026. He co-founded Dependabot (acquired by GitHub in 2019), was an early product and engineering lead at GoCardless (YC S11), and co-founded Pincites (YC S23, acquired by Filevine). The buildathon page still calls him "YC Partner." — [YC blog: Golda and Baker join as GPs](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/chris-golda-and-grey-baker-general-partners); [YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/people/grey-baker)
- Symphony 2026 is a "Voice AI in production" summit about "millions of customer calls" in Indian languages. It has three halls: SUR (main stage), ABHYAS (buildathon and hackathon) and MEHFIL (expo and live demos). Named speakers include Aarthi Ramamurthy (Schema Ventures, also a Bolna angel investor), Dr. Manish Gupta (Google DeepMind), Srinivas Narayanan (former OpenAI CTO of B2B Applications), Grey Baker (YC), Venky Balasubramaniam (Plivo CEO) and Sachin Bhatia (Exotel co-founder). — [Symphony page](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony)
- Symphony sponsors: Diamond tier is Google Cloud, **Truecaller for Business**, Exotel and Plivo. Gold is Kalpa Labs and Sarvam. Silver is Wispr Flow, Simplismart, Cartesia, Deepgram and Vobiz. Associates are Smallest.ai and Agnost AI. NVIDIA Inception and OpenAI are also listed. — [Symphony page](https://www.bolna.ai/symphony)
- Bolna's October 2026 changelog doesn't mention the buildathon. — [Oct 2026 changelog](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/changelog/october-2026.md)

### Inferences
- The panel is mixed: enterprise and telephony operators (Plivo, Exotel, Truecaller), a B2B SaaS investor (Aarthi Ramamurthy) and a YC GP with a developer-tools and B2B-SaaS background (Dependabot, Pincites). That mix probably rewards a **B2B product with a clear buyer, in production on Indian phone lines**, over a consumer novelty.
- Bolna does the shortlisting through interviews, so in practice the first gate is Bolna's own team. Builds that use Bolna deeply and could become a case study will likely do well.
- The summit theme is "voice AI in production," so a demo that runs on a real Indian phone number with real calls fits the room better than a browser-only demo.

### Gaps
- No published rubric, finale jury list or FAQ updates after Oct 15 were found. Ask in the Bolna Builders Slack.
- It's unclear whether Symphony's "hackathon," also on the ABHYAS stage, is a separate in-person event.

Enterprise-style judging, as at Google's Gen AI Exchange and IIT KGP's CallKaro "Golden Hour," explicitly rewards production-readiness, integration, guardrails and edge-case testing.

### Inferences
- Indian winners lean toward **"Bharat access" builds**: Indic, vernacular, voice-first users such as MSMEs, farmers, kids, state-board students and non-speakers. They have a clear underserved user and a live Indic demo. Pure novelty wins more at ElevenLabs-style global events.
- The voice-only Indian hackathons with enterprise sponsors (CallKaro and IIT KGP, Smallest.ai) judge on **engineering rigour for phone agents**: guardrails, tool calls, edge-case testing and latency. Bolna's shortlisting interviews will probably probe the same areas.
- B2B winners (MSME tenders, podcast ads) show a narrow buyer can win. Most winners, though, come from open briefs with a specific, concrete user.

## Q4. Bolna founders, investors, and the use cases they talk about

### Takeaway
Bolna was founded in 2024 by Maitreya Wagh (CEO; IIT Delhi, ex-Bain, Probo) and Prateek Sachan (CTO; ex-Zomato, Tata 1mg, BrowserStack, Atlassian). It is in **YC Fall 2025** (F25, confirmed), after five YC rejections, and raised a $6.3M seed led by General Catalyst in Jan 2026. Its money comes from **e-commerce, BFSI and education (about 80% of revenue)**. The use cases it advertises are reminders and collections, cart recovery, lead qualification, recruitment screening, support helplines and delivery confirmation. The founders frame voice AI as moving from "call-centre replacement" to **outcome-driven automation**, and their recurring line is "**Indian enterprises will pay**."

### Inferences
- The winning story, judged through Bolna's lens: a **B2B workflow in a high-volume Indian calling vertical** (BFSI collections, e-commerce RTO, cart and COD confirmation, recruitment screening, edtech leads, logistics and address capture, FMCG and distribution). The demo should show a **measurable outcome** (₹ recovered, hours saved, conversion), echoing the Hypothesis "₹2.5 Cr recovered" case study.
- Showing that **someone will pay** answers the objection YC raised to Bolna and gives Grey Baker a YC-style "who's the customer, what's the ROI" story. Letters of intent, pilot users or real call logs would be strong evidence.
- Bolna names **vernacular quality, noise and address recognition** as its own unsolved pain points. A build that visibly handles Kannada, Tamil or Bhojpuri, or noisy addresses, speaks directly to the founders' roadmap.
- The move to "complex multi-branch workflows, graph agents, tool calling" means a demo should look like an agent that **acts** (calls APIs, writes to a CRM, transfers to a human), not one that only chats.

## Q5. Bolna platform features new or showcased in 2026 that a winning project should show off

### Takeaway
Bolna's Sep–Oct 2026 changelog shows heavy investment in **graph agents with structured extraction, workflows and campaign webhooks, the web-search tool, SIP trunks and BYO carriers, Indic STT keyword biasing, Sarvam Bulbul v4 Flash, bring-your-own TTS, start-in-language, a Claude connector and MCP, and AI prompt editing**. A winning build should combine several of these into a working production loop.

### Cited Findings
- **October 2026**:
  - Oct 2: Sarvam `saaras:v4` keyword biasing (up to 50 terms).
  - Oct 7: AssemblyAI config and agent context in Hindi.
  - Oct 8: **Web search tool** (OpenAI, Exa, Firecrawl, Parallel).
  - Oct 8: **graph-agent extraction params with allowed values** and `node_extracted_data` in webhooks.
  - Oct 9: **Claude connector**. The full MCP server covers calls, numbers, KBs, SIP and workflows.
  - Oct 12: **Bring-your-own TTS** (OpenAI-speech-compatible, not billed by Bolna) and compliance-application APIs for buying Indian numbers.
  - Oct 15: **SIP trunks** with BYO carrier (Plivo, Twilio, Telnyx): inbound and outbound, DTMF, call transfer, carrier-header variables, 19 new MCP tools.
  - Oct 15: static nodes can end calls.
  - Oct 15: **Sarvam Bulbul v4 Flash**, 222 voices in 9 languages.
  - Source: [Oct 2026 changelog](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/changelog/october-2026.md)
- **September 2026**:
  - Sep 1: smarter multilingual language switching.
  - Sep 2: AWS Marketplace listing.
  - Sep 3: Kalpa Labs TTS (EN and HI).
  - Sep 4: extraction categories API and call-summary toggle.
  - Sep 9: AI edit of prompts with diffs.
  - Sep 20: keyword and context biasing across 7 STT providers.
  - Sep 22: **workflow execution webhooks**.
  - Sep 23: Soniox TTS and interruptible goodbye.
  - Sep 26: custom-LLM extra params.
  - Sep 29: ElevenLabs v4 Turbo and AssemblyAI Universal-3.
  - Sep 30: **start a call in a chosen language** (`agent_language`).
  - Source: [Sep 2026 changelog](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/changelog/september-2026.md)
- **Homepage-featured capabilities**: bulk calling at scale, custom API triggers during live calls, human-in-the-loop escalation, n8n, Make and Zapier integrations, per-call model switching, India or US data residency and on-prem, Agent Studio and Bolna CLI. — [bolna.ai](https://www.bolna.ai/)
- **Core positioning**: model-neutral orchestration over "every speech-to-text, every LLM, every text-to-speech, every telephony stack." — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/startups/how-bolna-ai-is-helping-enterprises-to-win-the-voice-ai-race/)

### Inferences
- **Features to show for maximum "Bolna fit"**:
  1. A graph agent with allowed-values extraction that feeds a webhook dashboard (structured outcomes).
  2. A Workflow or batch campaign with execution webhooks (scale).
  3. Custom function calls plus call transfer to a human (action and HITL).
  4. A vernacular start language with Sarvam saaras v4 keyword biasing and Bulbul v4 Flash (Indic quality, plus sponsor Sarvam).
  5. A real Indian number via Plivo or Exotel, or a SIP trunk (sponsors and production realism).
  6. Optionally the web search tool, DTMF for long numbers, and agent creation through the MCP or Claude connector, which shows developer velocity to a YC GP from a developer-tools background.
- Truecaller for Business is a Diamond Symphony sponsor and Bolna advertises Truecaller verification, so caller-ID or trust features fit the theme. That fit is inferred, not stated by Bolna.

### Gaps
- I didn't separately confirm the Web Call SDK (browser voice) and its 2026 status in this pass. The local `ideas-top5.md` references Bolna's SDK docs, but Q5 fetches covered only the Sep and Oct changelogs.
- There's no official statement on whether judges score sponsor-tech usage.
