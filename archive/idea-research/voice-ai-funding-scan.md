# Scan: funded voice-AI startups to "copy, then make our own" (2026-10-18)

> Method: web search of 2026 funding news and YC batch listings. Figures are mostly from aggregators and secondary reports (🟡) unless noted, and the scan isn't exhaustive. Verify a figure before putting it in the pitch.

## The market
- About **$1.2B** raised by voice-AI companies in 2026 up to September, vs $585M in the same period of 2025 ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/sectors/voice-ai/__ooDO2Zv7I7Lr6Wb6_VbfEVpA8rhLHYzpKzl-EEZFlGw)).

## Conclusion
1. **No better copy-and-twist candidate turned up.** Most of the money goes to infrastructure (which competes with Bolna) or to sales, support and receptionist agents (Bolna's customers already do these).
2. **The strongest signal is for our own engine.** Investors are funding AI voice *interviewers* for customers (Listen Labs, Outset, Koji, Perspective) **and for employees (Sona8, YC F26)**.
3. **Pitch framing to adopt:** *"We're building the AI interviewer that gets out what people know. We start with churned customers (revenue at risk today), then win-loss, then employee knowledge."* That's one engine for three markets, each with a funded comparable.
4. **Parked for after Oct 31:** India insurance-claim calls (SuperDial-style) for hospitals.
