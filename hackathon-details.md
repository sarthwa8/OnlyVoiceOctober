# Bolna Buildathon @ Symphony 2026

> Source: https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon (fetched 2026-10-18)

**Tagline:** "Bring any idea. Give it a voice." / "Build anything. Just make it talk."
**Headline perk:** Path to YC — the winning team gets a Y Combinator interview.

## TL;DR

| | |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Fully virtual (build from anywhere) |

## What to build

- Anything, as long as **voice is part of it**.
- Can be a **new product** or a **voice feature on something you've already built** (or something in between).
- No tracks.

**Example ideas from the page:**
1. A voice tutor for kids
2. A phone helpdesk for a local clinic
3. A voice layer on an app you already built
4. A hands-free tool for drivers or field workers

## Registration (Apply form)

Apply at: https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon#apply-form

Fields:
- **Your team:** Team size (Just me / 2 / 3 / 4)
- **About you:** Full name, Email, Phone (WhatsApp), City, College or company, LinkedIn or GitHub
- **Your idea:**
  - Have you decided on your idea? (Yes / Not yet)
  - Project name
  - Since when have you been working on this idea?
  - What are you building, and where does voice AI come in?
- Consent checkbox: agree to be contacted by Bolna about the Buildathon and Symphony

## Project submission (due Oct 31)

Submit at: https://www.bolna.ai/symphony/buildathon/submit
"Share what you built. Selected teams move to interview."

Fields:
- **Team size** (Just me / 2 / 3 / 4)
- **Full name**
- **Email** (use the same email you registered with)
- **Project name**
- **Project description** (1–2 lines on what it does and who it's for, **max 200 characters**)
- **Submission link:** GitHub repo, live app (Vercel etc.) or demo link
- **Pitch deck or pitch video link:** Google Drive or Luma link, with sharing set to **"Anyone with the link"**

### Deliverables checklist
- [ ] Register via the apply form
- [ ] Working project with a voice component
- [ ] GitHub repo and/or live deployed app / demo link
- [ ] Pitch deck **or** pitch video (shared publicly via link)
- [ ] Description of 200 characters or fewer
- [ ] Submit before Oct 31

## Selection process

1. Bolna reviews every submission and shortlists the strongest builds.
2. Shortlisted teams get an email invite (Nov 3) for an interview (Nov 5–7).
3. Finalists are announced Nov 8. The top 10 get Symphony tickets and the top 3 pitch at the finale.
4. Winner is decided at the Nov 20 Grand Finale.

No explicit judging rubric is published on the page.

---

## FAQs

- **Who can apply?** Students, professionals and indie builders: anyone who wants to build with voice.
- **Is the Buildathon in person?** No, it's fully virtual. Only the top 3 teams come to Abhyas Hall, The Lalit Ashok, Bengaluru on 20th Nov to present at Symphony, with travel covered.
- **Can I apply as an individual?** Yes. Solo, or a team of up to 4.
- **What kind of builds are allowed?** Anything with voice in it: a new product, a voice feature on an existing one, or something in between.
- **How does the selection process work?** Every application is reviewed and the strongest builds are shortlisted. Selected teams hear back by email.
- **Will travel be covered for the finalists?** Yes, for the top 3 teams.
- **Will we get tickets to Symphony?** Yes. The top 10 teams get free tickets.
